Welcome to Grok Desk

An unofficial macOS client for Grok Build · Unnotarized preview

  1. Install. Drag Grok Desk.app onto the Applications folder in this disk image.
  2. Try opening it. Open Grok Desk from Applications. If macOS says the developer cannot be verified or Apple cannot check the app, dismiss that alert.
  3. Choose whether to trust this preview. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security. Scroll to Security and find the notice for Grok Desk. If you trust this download, click Open Anyway and complete macOS’s confirmation yourself.

Open Privacy & Security

The shortcut only opens Settings; it does not grant approval. If your macOS version or browser does not open the pane, use the System Settings shortcut in the disk image and choose Privacy & Security manually. The notice appears after an attempted launch; managed Macs may restrict this option.

Why the extra step?

This preview is locally signed but is not Developer ID signed or notarized by Apple. It cannot approve itself. Do not override a warning that the app is damaged or will harm your computer; download a fresh copy or report the problem.

Your own Grok account

Grok Build is a separate prerequisite. This installer does not download the CLI or include an account, keys, or login information. Follow the repository’s setup instructions to install Grok Build and sign in.
